How to set up your iOS App Icon:

1. Place your main high-resolution PNG icon (1024x1024 px recommended) into this folder:
   ios/ARTraceAndDraw/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/

2. Name the file:
   icon-1024.png

3. Alternatively, in Xcode, open ARTraceAndDraw.xcodeproj, click on Assets.xcassets -> AppIcon, and drag & drop your icon image directly into the AppIcon slot.

Xcode will automatically generate all necessary device scales from icon-1024.png.
